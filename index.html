How it works

Three steps from
showroom to your floor.

Velura reads your room the way a stone fitter would — where the floor is, how far it runs, where the light falls — then lays the slab you chose, exactly as it would be installed.

Your room photo
01

Upload your room

Take a photo from standing height. Velura finds the floor automatically, on your device. Refine the edges with a brush if you like.

02

Choose your stone

160 natural stones — marble, onyx, travertine, quartzite. Pick the format, the pattern and the finish: polished, honed or leathered.

03

See it laid

The real slab, tile by tile, in true perspective with your room’s own light and reflections. Compare before and after, save, share.

Velura uses real photographs of each stone and lays them at true size — a 120 × 120 cm tile is 120 × 120 cm on your floor. Natural stone varies slab to slab, so always see the actual slab before you buy; Velura gets you to the right stone faster.
